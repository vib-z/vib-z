Sheet music for talking.

Plain text keeps the words and throws away the voice. This is the same sentence with the voice written back in: height is pitch, size is volume, stretch is time, color is stress, space is a pause. Drag the slider to bring the voice in, then press play to read it the way a teleprompter would flow it.

A sketch of Idea 001: one codified prosody layer on plain English — readable by people, playable by machines. In the real thing the scoring is automatic (from a recording) or planned (principles as code); here it is hand-scored to JFK's actual delivery, including the famous drawn-out, dropped-low hard.
